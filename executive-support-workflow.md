# Executive Support Workflow

## Role Overview
This workflow demonstrates how executive and administrative support tasks are managed in a structured remote environment.

## Core Responsibilities
- Calendar scheduling
- Email management
- Meeting coordination
- Task tracking
- Research and documentation support

## Daily Workflow

### 1. Morning Planning
- Review calendar for the day
- Prioritize urgent tasks
- Check emails and respond to time-sensitive messages

### 2. Task Management
- Organize tasks in a tracker (Notion/Trello)
- Update progress on ongoing activities
- Flag blocked or urgent items

### 3. Communication Support
- Respond to emails and messages
- Schedule meetings with stakeholders
- Send reminders for deadlines and meetings

### 4. Meeting Support
- Prepare agenda before meetings
- Join or coordinate meetings
- Document key points and action items

### 5. End-of-Day Wrap-Up
- Update task list
- Send follow-up emails
- Prepare next-day schedule

## Tools Used
- Google Calendar
- Gmail
- Notion / Trello
- Zoom
- Slack

## Outcome
Improved organization, better time management, and efficient remote executive support operations.
